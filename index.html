The learned rulebook

The agent's whole “brain” is a 360-row table. Each cell is the irrigation it chooses for that situation. rare = seen fewer than 50 times in training.

What it figured out on its own

  1. Wait until about 60% of the soil water is used. The FAO-56 rule says 55%, and nobody told it.
  2. Dry soil and no rain coming: give the full 40 mm.
  3. Heavy rain in the forecast: irrigate less or not at all.

Module 1 · Reinforcement learning for irrigation

Environment
Daily FAO-56 soil-water balance driven by real weather (Pune, Jodhpur, Ludhiana, 2010–2025); FAO-33 yield.
State
Soil dryness (10) × crop stage (4) × 3-day rain forecast (3) × ET0 (3) = 360 states.
Actions
Irrigate 0 / 10 / 20 / 30 / 40 mm today.
Reward
−(water + 10 × crop water shortage + water drained + 10 per irrigation event).
Algorithm
Tabular Q-learning, ε 1→0.05, α 0.1→0.01, γ 0.95, 50,000 seasons per crop.
Baselines
Rainfed · fixed 40 mm every 7 days · FAO soil-sensor threshold rule.

Module 2 · Deep learning for leaf disease

Data
PlantVillage colour images, maize + tomato, 14 classes, 70/15/15 split.
Model
MobileNetV2 pretrained on ImageNet → global average pool → linear classifier (transfer learning).
Explainability
Class activation map: classifier weights × last convolutional feature maps.
Deployment
Exported to ONNX and run on your device with ONNX Runtime Web. No server, no upload.
